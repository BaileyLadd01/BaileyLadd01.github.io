AspenAutoKit

A high-throughput Python framework for automating Aspen Plus, and the enhanced carbon capture model it was built to calibrate.

The paper

AspenAutoKit: A Comprehensive, High-Throughput Python-COM Automation Framework for the Development and Utilisation of an Enhanced Carbon Capture Model in Aspen Plus

Vincent Bailey Ladd, Jack Wells, Georgeos Hardo, Derek Ingham, Lin Ma, Mohamed Pourkashanian

Presented at
GHGT-18, Perth, October 2026
Status
Conference paper

In brief

Calibrating a rate-based amine capture model means searching tens of thousands of packing configurations and tuning factors, far more than can be tried by hand. AspenAutoKit ran more than 25,000 simulations in parallel and generated the statistics behind every calibration decision automatically.

The resulting model cut the specific reboiler duty error against pilot-plant measurements from 0.454 to 0.150 MJ/kg CO2 and halved the stripper temperature error, then mapped out operating conditions for 30 mol.% CO2 flue gas, beyond anything the plant has yet tested.
